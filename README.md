## About Me

I am a Computer Science researcher specializing in **Machine Learning and Data Mining**, with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Speech Processing, and Language Technologies.

My research focuses on **Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems for low-resource languages**, particularly Tunisian Arabic, exploring how pretrained speech synthesis models can be adapted under limited data and computational resources.

I am interested in developing efficient and accessible AI solutions for underrepresented languages, bridging research and practical applications.

- 🎓 **Academic Background:** Master's Degree in Computer Science – Machine Learning & Data Mining.
- 🔬 **Research Interests:** Speech Synthesis, Speech Processing, Deep Learning, Low-Resource NLP.
- 🗣️ **Current Research Focus:** TTS for Tunisian Arabic and performance prediction of pretrained speech synthesis models.
- 🌍 **International Experience:** Erasmus+ KA171 academic mobility in France.
- 📄 **Research:** Submitted a research paper to ICASSP 2027.
- 💻 **GitHub:** [github.com/mohameddhouib5](https://github.com/mohameddhouib5)

## Featured Research

**Performance Prediction for Low-Resource Text-to-Speech Systems**

Research focused on predicting the adaptation quality of pretrained TTS models for low-resource languages, with an application to Tunisian Arabic.

Key research directions:
- Evaluating pretrained TTS architectures.
- Investigating the impact of training data quantity.
- Comparing speech synthesis quality and intelligibility.
- Developing predictive approaches for model selection under resource constraints.
